## 2. Precisely where direct planar cover applies

`Compact/Core/PlanarVertex.lean` proves the continuous statement for a bounded
closed polygon outside finitely many open convex blocks. A target nonstrict
affine bound follows by checking all feasible intersections of nonparallel
boundary lines. Degenerate segments and points are included. No arrangement
cells or cut-tree ancestry need to be serialized.

`Compact/Bridge/DirectPoseCover.lean` currently specializes this to the
original `poseInputs` and point-derived `poseForbidden`. Its output is one
convex reduced pose, or an empty root, per angle root. `DirectPoseForms`
derives exact integer rows from the original formulas and selected indices.

That gives three concrete gates before deleting another case's initial tree:

1. **Premises:** derive its actual inside/outside points, frame and complete
   closed angle cover from the original packing and ownership branch.
2. **Geometry:** identify all forbidden blocks. The existing point bridge
   does not automatically include foreign compulsory-hull overlap blocks,
   branch center cuts or previously derived strict-point updates. Their
   existing soundness may be reused, but must be composed explicitly with
   the general planar theorem.
3. **Output:** check the exact required reduced domain. Multiple disjoint
   targets at one angle do not become covered merely by proving the bounds
   of their convex hull. A convex enlargement is useful only if the later
   proof is verified on that enlargement. Nonconstant strict target rows
   also require more than the present closed-relaxation bound.

For point-only entrances, the own-point inequalities share two axes, so four
extreme point bounds and the four container rows suffice as a base. With O
excluded points there are at most `8 + 4*O` boundary rows before deduplication.
Thus the finite arithmetic can be bounded by the number of owner/query/angle
signatures, while source records contain selected inputs and shared target IDs.
This is an initial-cover bound, not a bound on later source-pose × neighbor-pose
checks. No audit yet proves that every late-case target passes this replacement.

## 6. A constructive budget test, without guessed allocations

The strongest defensible plan is to make the present retained delivery fit
the 300k target first, then bound each new whole-case increment before it is
merged. The C898 shared-point route is the immediate candidate for removing
its approximately 20,820 lines of old pair-check boilerplate, while retaining
the explicit fallbacks; that source saving must be measured after emission
and must not be subtracted in advance. The six-case shared-point audit tests
whether the same *mathematical* reduction removes most of its 639,066 repeated
pair obligations before a large Lean table is generated.

For that extension, the source representation is explicit original plan and
neighbor arrays plus at most 100 half-open Cartesian groups per stage. A
group carries either one rational point with all four own-face checks for
every participating context, or the unchanged eight-axis fallback relation.
The 113-stage outer limit is therefore 11,300 groups. This is a bound on
group descriptions only, not on the work or source of unresolved chord,
relaxation, entrance and late-case arguments. The 21 actual empty-context
closures must remain certified separately. No pair disappears because a
candidate point search failed.

After a genuine full preferred source census gives size L, the remaining
budget is `1,000,000 - L`, against which the following new objects must be
measured once. A credible bound requires their complete unions, not an
average from an easy pilot. In particular, the 41 remaining baseline cases,
C976's largest residuals, the C977/C981 surviving DAGs and mathematically
missing C983/C1090–1092 objects currently prevent that accounting. Reserving
an arbitrary allowance for them would not resolve the gap.

For each retained proof route, the countable objects are:

1. Actual ownership/symmetry/angle entrances and their direct-cover roots.
2. Distinct affine rows, poses, compulsory hulls and forbidden blocks.
3. Support contexts, exact basis memberships/fans and exceptional sparse
   witnesses; coefficient byte lengths matter as well as counts.
4. Pruning cuts or replacement universal bounds; outgoing relaxation links;
   chord/window/strict-point updates; every actual branch and terminal.
5. The shared soundness, original-model adapters and final proof composition.

Count the exact transitive union once, with a deterministic readable emitter,
and retain a complete reference-resolution report. Each unrecognized schema
or missing antecedent is an explicit unbounded item. A forest disappears only
after the direct cover proves the required target; a pair table disappears
only after its complete cached-fan checks or a whole symbolic implication
replace it. Source compression must not hide an equivalent huge payload in
one string or line.

The next measurements needed to make a sub-million claim credible are:

* Accept the complete cyclic fan tables and pair bindings, then discharge the
  exact 113 pair/21 chord-stage graph contracts against the one 143,532-line
  bank. Implement or symbolically replace all required pruning/relaxation
  implications and entrances. Measure one whole accepted case and the final
  seven-case union; graph fields alone do not answer the 300k question.
* Produce one complete hard C976 residual using the direct/hull entrance and
  shared support representation, while separately counting **all eleven**
  terminal-reachable object closures. A successful smallest-vector pilot alone
  cannot bound vector44 or51.
* Count the surviving C977/C981 DAGs after the already proved symbolic cuts,
  including refreshes and all split children. Prove a valid common terminal
  entrance before removing its former tail witnesses.
* Obtain complete objects for the currently mathematically open routes,
  especially C983 coverage and C1090–1092. No finite source budget follows
  while those objects themselves are absent.
* Count and cover every one of the 41 remaining baseline contracts, then add
  the actual shared union to the late-case and preservation counts.
