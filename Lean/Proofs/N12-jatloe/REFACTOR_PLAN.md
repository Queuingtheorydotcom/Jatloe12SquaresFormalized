## Next mathematical reductions with the highest payoff

The direct initial cover resolves a major representation problem, but its
6,848 checks are not 6,848 newly solved cases. The next work should close
whole original contracts or remove repeated mathematical work from their
complete derivations:

1. **Share directional support across pair obligations.** A pair step asks
   repeatedly for extrema of the same pose polygon in the same families of
   directions. Prove a polygon's valid two-row support once and reuse it at
   every requesting pair, retaining strictness and actual row membership.
   The exact C898 audit found 10,072 context/basis memberships behind 413,488
   repeated basis occurrences. The prepared support-fan pilot is a concrete
   route; the full seven-case savings and replay cost remain unmeasured.
   Where a stage's pair list is exactly a Cartesian coverage relation,
   derive that relation from the domain lists rather than store every pair.
   This requires an exhaustive coverage proof, not merely a shorter selector.
2. **Finish C713 as a second complete compact exclusion.** Its ten-step
   historical trace and now-audited direct entrance make it the strongest
   independent preservation test. Retain the actual corner/wall/central
   coupling and terminal. Work backward from required terminal inequalities
   to prune unused rows and transitions, but retain a well-founded dependency
   graph and every necessary neighbor and branch. This should measure the
   remaining downstream cost rather than produce another isolated helper.
3. **Replace merged-central baseline families jointly.** The original cases
   `71,80,589,590` and `48,573,574` have adjacent central owners; the checked
   `CentralPairPoint` theorem supplies new compulsory points. Their missing
   whole-family lemma must combine those points with the actual wall/corner
   owners. The existing four-square theorem has false missing-marker premises
   on these inputs, so relabeling it is not a solution. A complete coupled
   argument could remove seven original obligations with one theorem.
4. **Uniform terminals for C977/C981 before more context traces.** C977 now
   has four wall branches across 57 vectors; prove shared branch implications
   parameterized by the actual compulsory/excluded points instead of writing
   228 independent geometric traces. C981's uniform center cuts similarly
   need a genuine coupled terminal and original catalogue coverage. The known
   counterexample to its missing left-wall barrier must remain respected.
   These are research targets, not existing exclusions or budget savings.

The first two routes have exact input audits and accepted generic mathematics
behind them; the latter two still require new coupled proofs. Progress toward
a sub-million-line result should be credited only after whole-case acceptance
and measured dependency unions. A complete C713 pilot plus the seven-case
support inventory would provide much stronger budget evidence than another
small local certificate.

## Budget gates and resource policy

The earlier measured source union is 401 local modules, 177,823 Lean lines and
16,659,574 bytes. Its roots are the symbolic registry, complete C898 source,
C981 V116 and the C977/C981 wall-center adapters. This is a provisional source
snapshot, not a 300,000-line preservation of all previously proved work.
It predates the direct-cover replacement. The new C898 initial-cover emitter
reports 26 modules, 25,386 lines and 1,097,644 source bytes, with 7,864 target
row records and 5,632 selected input indices. It derives 26,624 input/forbidden
rows rather than serializing them. These are replacement-component counts,
not a new whole-project total, and the new component is still being replayed.

Use the remaining-proof plan's proposed allocations as stop-and-reassess
limits: 50,000 lines for shared foundations and symbolic assembly, 250,000
additional lines for all seven historical exclusions, and a 950,000-line
planned total including contingency. None is a forecast or an established
upper bound. Count shared dependencies once, and record bytes, semantic
records, object sizes, checking time and memory alongside lines. Dense literals,
long lines and external generated archives do not constitute simplification.

The current serial compiler pilot has independent caps of 1,800 MiB aggregate
RSS and 800 MiB physical footprint, with time and free-disk limits and process
group cleanup. Use the external guard; do not add an internal `-M` flag. Separate
numerical jobs remain bounded to 128–384 MiB. No competing compiler or generator
runs are allowed. Keep total local storage below 10 GB and report external
library compilation/cache costs separately.

The global size and time risks are unresolved: complete acceptance of the
written source, actual compact cost of the recovered seven, unsupported replay
operations, and the coupled geometry/coverage gaps in the other cases. There is
no evidence-based completion ETA or justified sub-million-line guarantee yet.
